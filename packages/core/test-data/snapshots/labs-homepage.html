Frequently Asked Questions

What shipping options do you offer?

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $50, with delivery in 3-5 business days. Express shipping is available for $9.99 with 1-2 business day delivery. All orders include tracking and insurance.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked return policy on all unworn items. Simply initiate a return through your account dashboard, print the prepaid label, and drop off the package. Refunds are processed within 48 hours of receiving the return.

How do I find the right shoe size?

Use our interactive size guide available on every product page. Measure your foot length in centimeters and match it to our conversion chart. If you are between sizes, we recommend going up half a size for running shoes and staying true to size for casual shoes.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to over 40 countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Most international orders arrive within 7-14 business days.
